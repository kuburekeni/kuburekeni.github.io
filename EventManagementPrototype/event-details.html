About this event

Join us for the biggest outdoor music celebration of the year. Featuring headliners from rock, pop, electronic and indie scenes, food trucks, art installations and more. Suitable for all ages.

Gates open at 13:00. Please bring valid ID. Rain or shine. Limited parking — public transport recommended.
