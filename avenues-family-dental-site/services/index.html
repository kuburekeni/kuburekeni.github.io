Our treatments

General, preventative and cosmetic dentistry for the whole family, in one practice in central Ashgrove.

Preventative dentistry

Regular check-ups catch problems while they're still small and cheap to fix.

Children's dentistry

Bulk billed for eligible kids through the Medicare Child Dental Benefits Schedule.

Fillings

Tooth-coloured materials and gentler techniques than the ones you remember.

Crowns, bridges and veneers

Rebuilding a damaged tooth so it looks and works like the rest of them.

Root canal treatment

Saving a badly infected tooth instead of pulling it out.

Wisdom teeth extraction

Assessment, removal, and honest advice on whether they need to come out at all.

Dental implants

A permanent replacement for a missing tooth that doesn't rely on the teeth beside it.

Dentures

Full and partial dentures, fitted and adjusted until they're comfortable.

Clear aligners

Straightening crooked teeth without fixed braces.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening, with realistic expectations set before you start.

Custom mouthguards

Fitted for footy season, and no gap with eligible cover.

Occlusal splints and snoring devices

For grinding, jaw pain and snoring — appliances made to fit you.

Happy gas

For nervous patients and anyone who'd rather not remember the appointment.

IV sedation

Deeper sedation for longer procedures or significant dental anxiety.
