As a TUH preferred provider, Teachers Union Health members with eligible dental cover receive a higher rebate here than at a non-preferred practice.

No gap check-up and clean

Teachers Union Health (TUH) members with eligible dental cover pay no gap* for a check-up and clean with us — that's the full examination, digital X-rays where required, and a professional scale and clean.

How claiming works

We claim through HICAPS at the front desk. Swipe your Teachers Union Health (TUH) card, the rebate comes off immediately, and you only pay the difference — there's nothing to claim back afterwards.

Cover varies between policies. Call us on (07) 3366 6444 before your appointment and we'll check what your particular Teachers Union Health (TUH) policy covers.
