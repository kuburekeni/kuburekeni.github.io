The Avenues Family Dental believes in making our Ashgrove dentist services affordable. We work with a range of private health funds to help reduce out-of-pocket costs for our patients.

What's included

  • Full dental examination
  • Digital X-rays where required
  • Professional scale and clean

Without health cover, the same appointment is $240.

Who's eligible

We are unique in that we offer no gap fees* for check-ups for all eligible health fund members. For patients with private health cover that includes dental, you may pay nothing out of pocket for your examination and clean, depending on your level of cover.

Our health fund partners include Bupa, Medibank, TUH, HCF and NIB — including NIB DentalPass members and NIB partner funds: AAMI Health Insurance, Apia Health Insurance, Suncorp Health Insurance, Qantas Health Insurance, ING Health Insurance, Priceline Health Insurance, GU Health, IMAN Australian Health Plans, United Healthcare Global, Real Health Insurance and Seniors Health Insurance.

We recommend checking your policy details, or speaking with our team, to confirm what your cover includes before your appointment. Call (07) 3366 6444 and we'll check for you.

Don't forget we're open late on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday evenings until 9pm, and Saturday mornings.
