Teeth whitening

Professional whitening lifts staining that brushing can't. It works well on some kinds of discolouration and not on others.

Teeth darken over time, and coffee, tea, red wine and tobacco all speed that along. Professional whitening uses a controlled bleaching agent to lift that staining.

We start with an examination. Whitening works on natural tooth enamel — it won't change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, so if you have restorations at the front of your mouth, that affects the plan.

It's also worth knowing that discolouration coming from inside a tooth, rather than from surface staining, responds differently and may need another approach.

We'll tell you before you start what result is realistically achievable for your teeth.
