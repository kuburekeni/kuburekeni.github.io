Fees and health fund cover

We claim on the spot through HICAPS for every private health fund, and we're a preferred provider for five of them.

Fees and cover

  • No gap check-up & clean
  • Bupa preferred provider
  • Medibank preferred provider
  • HCF preferred provider
  • NIB preferred provider
  • TUH preferred provider

The short version

A complete check-up and clean is $240, or no gap* if you have eligible private health cover. That covers the full examination, digital X-rays where required, and a professional scale and clean.

We claim through HICAPS at the front desk, so you pay the difference on the day rather than claiming it back yourself.

Preferred provider funds

We're a preferred provider for five funds, which means members get a higher rebate here than at a non-preferred practice:

  • Bupa
  • Medibank
  • HCF
  • NIB
  • Teachers Union Health (TUH)

NIB cover includes DentalPass members and NIB partner funds: AAMI Health Insurance, Apia Health Insurance, Suncorp Health Insurance, Qantas Health Insurance, ING Health Insurance, Priceline Health Insurance, GU Health, IMAN Australian Health Plans, United Healthcare Global, Real Health Insurance and Seniors Health Insurance.

Other ways to pay

  • Medicare CBDS — we bulk bill eligible children through the Child Dental Benefits Schedule.
  • Department of Veterans' Affairs — we treat all DVA patients.
  • Zip and Afterpay — pay in instalments if that suits you better.
  • All other health funds — on-the-spot HICAPS claiming, whichever fund you're with.

Not sure what your policy covers? Call us on (07) 3366 6444 before you book and we'll check what you're entitled to.

*No gap includes check-ups, scale & cleans and routine X-rays where required, provided you have dental cover with a rebate associated with the item numbers. Some patients may not receive the maximum benefit depending on their level of cover and available entitlements.
