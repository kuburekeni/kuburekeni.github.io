    Know what you'll pay before you sit down

    We claim on the spot through HICAPS for every private health fund, so you only ever pay the difference. For five funds we're a preferred provider, which means a bigger rebate and a smaller bill.

    We also bulk bill eligible children through the Medicare Child Dental Benefits Schedule, treat all Department of Veterans' Affairs patients, and accept Zip and Afterpay if you'd rather pay in instalments.
