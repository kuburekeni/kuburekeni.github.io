Our philosophy

How we like to work, and what you can expect from an appointment here.

Our vision is to be a leader in patient service. Our experienced team achieves that by being professional, punctual, relaxed, gentle, good value and contemporary.

We explain before we treat

You should never leave an appointment unsure about what was done or why. We'll show you what we're looking at, describe the options including the option of waiting, and tell you what each one costs before anything starts.

We run on time

Your time matters. We book realistically rather than stacking appointments, and if we're running late we'll tell you rather than leave you guessing in the waiting room.

We don't do guilt

Plenty of people come to us after years away from a dentist, often because of a bad experience somewhere else. Nobody here will lecture you about it. We'll just work out where things stand and what to do next.

We're open when you're free

Late nights until 9pm on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, plus Saturday mornings. Dental care shouldn't require taking leave.

We keep the cost visible

A check-up and clean is $240, or no gap* with eligible cover. For anything more involved you'll get a written estimate before you decide. See how fees and health fund cover work.

Sixty-five years in Ashgrove

We've been on Waterworks Road since 1961. Come and see whether we suit you.
