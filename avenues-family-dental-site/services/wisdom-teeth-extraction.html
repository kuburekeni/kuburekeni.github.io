Wisdom teeth extraction

Not every wisdom tooth needs removing. We'll assess yours and tell you plainly whether it's causing a problem.

Wisdom teeth usually appear in the late teens or early twenties. Where there's room for them and they come through cleanly, they can be left alone.

Problems arise when there isn't enough space. A partially erupted or impacted wisdom tooth can trap food and bacteria, cause repeated infections, damage the tooth in front of it, or simply hurt.

We'll take an X-ray and show you what's actually going on before recommending anything. If removal is the right call, we'll explain what's involved, what recovery looks like, and what it will cost.

Happy gas and IV sedation are both available if you'd rather not be fully present for it.
