The Avenues Family Dental sees many children as patients. Promoting good oral hygiene habits at as young an age as possible is one of the most valuable things you can do for your child's long-term health.

First visits are deliberately low-pressure. We count teeth, take a look around, and let your child get comfortable with the chair — often that first appointment involves no treatment at all.

Eligible children can be bulk billed through the Medicare Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CBDS), which covers a set amount of basic dental services over a two-year period. If you're not sure whether your child qualifies, call us and we can check for you.

Our late nights until 9pm on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, plus Saturday mornings, mean appointments don't have to come out of school hours.
