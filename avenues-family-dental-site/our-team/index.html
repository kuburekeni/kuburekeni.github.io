Our dentists

Ten dentists, trained across Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, El Salvador, the UK and Russia. If you've found someone you get on with, you can book with them by name.

Dr Peter Allen

BDSc (Qld)

Peter started private practice at Strathpine and practised there for 31 years. In 2000 he bought the Ashgrove practice and continues to practise there today. His goal in practice is simply to give the best dental care possible.

Dr Joanne Mattner

BDSc (Qld)

Joanne graduated with a Bachelor of Dental Science from the University of Queensland in 1989 and has been providing quality dental care in The Gap and Newmarket areas since 1997.

Dr Vanessa Romer

BDS (El Salvador) — fluent in Spanish

Vanessa graduated from the Evangelical University School of Dentistry in 2004, worked in one of El Salvador's largest regional hospitals, and completed a Diploma of Dental Technology in Brisbane in 2010.

Dr Miriam Tane

BDS (Otago)

Miriam graduated from the University of Otago in 2004, practised with Queensland Health for two years, and has worked alongside Dr Peter Allen in Ashgrove and Bray Park since.

Dr Annette Gareis

Dentist

Annette graduated in Dentistry from the University of Stellenbosch, South Africa, and has practised in the UK, South Africa and — since 2001 — Australia, across both NSW and Queensland.

Dr Mick Curtis

BSc (UNSW), BDent (USyd), GDipClinDent (UniMelb)

Mick is a general dentist with over ten years' experience and postgraduate qualifications in dental implants.

Dr Jungin Park

BDS (Otago)

Jungin graduated from the University of Otago in 2003 and has a particular interest in difficult tooth extractions.

Dr Nick White

BSc (UQ), BDent (USyd)

Nick is a skilled and compassionate dentist with over 20 years' experience, having completed a Bachelor of Science at UQ before his dental studies at the University of Sydney.

Dr Steve Ceccato

Dentist

A proud Brisbane local who grew up in the area and attended Marist College Ashgrove just down the road, Steve builds long-lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr Anastasia Salynskaya

FRACDS (GDP)

Anastasia graduated in Moscow in 2010 with a two-year postgraduate residency in oral surgery, and achieved her Fellowship in General Dental Practice with the RACDS in 2022.
